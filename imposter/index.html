How to play
  1. Pass the phone around. Each person taps to see their secret word — one of you is the imposter and won't get it.
  2. Going in order, everyone says one word or short hint about the secret word. Don't be too obvious!
  3. The imposter has to bluff and guess what the word is.
  4. Talk, then vote out who you think is the imposter.
  5. Tap Reveal. Group wins if they catch the imposter — imposter wins if they survive, or if they guess the word.
